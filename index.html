About Me

Where strategy
meets storytelling

I'm a Las Vegas-based communications professional with a deep passion for brands that connect people to experiences they'll never forget. With a background spanning public relations, social media, event coordination, and content strategy, I've spent over seven years helping brands across hospitality, lifestyle, and entertainment find their voice and amplify it. Today, much of my work is with nonprofit and education clients, where clear, human storytelling helps organizations reach the people they serve.

Whether I'm crafting a story for earned media, coordinating a live event, or developing a social campaign from the ground up, I show up with intention, creativity, and a commitment to results.
